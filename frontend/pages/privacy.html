Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Attachment Record Management System (ARMS) collects, why it is collected, and who can see it.

Information we collect

  • Account details: name, email address, phone number and password. Passwords are stored in hashed form and cannot be read by staff.
  • For students: registration number, course and year of study.
  • For supervisors: department.
  • Attachment details: organization, department, location, company supervisor contact details, and start and end dates.
  • Records created while using ARMS: logbook entries, supervisor comments and approval decisions, evaluations, generated reports and notifications.
  • Password reset requests: a one-time reset token, stored in hashed form, and the email address it was sent to.

How we use it

  • To let students record and report on their industrial attachment.
  • To let supervisors review logbook entries and evaluate students.
  • To let administrators assign supervisors and manage accounts.
  • To send a password reset email when you ask for one.

Who can see it

  • Students can see their own records only.
  • Supervisors can see the records of the students assigned to them.
  • Administrators can see student and supervisor accounts and attachment assignments.
  • Your information is not sold or shared with advertisers.

Storage and security

Records are stored in the ARMS database. Access requires a login, and sessions are held in your browser only until you log out or close the tab. Reset links expire after one hour and work once.

Keeping and deleting your information

Records are kept for as long as your account exists. An administrator can delete an account, which also deletes that student's attachment, logbook, evaluation and report records.

Your choices and contact

ARMS is run for the University of Kabianga. To ask for your information to be corrected or deleted, or to ask any question about this policy, contact the University through its contact page.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated.
