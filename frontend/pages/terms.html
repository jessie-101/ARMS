Terms and Conditions

Last updated: 10 November 2026

By creating an account or logging in to the Attachment Record Management System (ARMS), you agree to these terms.

Accounts

  • You must give accurate details when you register.
  • Keep your password private. You are responsible for activity under your account.
  • Each person may hold one account.

Logbook and records

  • Students must write logbook entries truthfully and only for weekdays within their attachment period.
  • Supervisors must review entries and give evaluations honestly and only for students assigned to them.
  • An entry that has been approved or rejected cannot be reviewed again.

Acceptable use

  • Do not try to access another person's account or records.
  • Do not upload or enter false, offensive or unlawful content.
  • Do not try to disrupt or damage the system.

Suspension

Administrators may suspend or delete an account that breaks these terms.

Availability

ARMS is provided as an academic record-keeping tool. We try to keep it available but cannot guarantee it will always be free of interruptions or errors. Keep your own copy of any record you cannot afford to lose.

Your information

How your information is handled is described in the Privacy Policy.

Contact

ARMS is run for the University of Kabianga. For questions about these terms, use the University's contact page.

Changes to these terms

If these terms change, the date at the top of this page will be updated. Continuing to use ARMS after a change means you accept the new terms.
