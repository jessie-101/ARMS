Attachment Record Management System

Students write a logbook entry for each weekday of their 12-week attachment. Supervisors review and comment on every entry, then evaluate the student at the end.

How ARMS works

Each role has its own portal.

Students

Add your placement details, write your daily logbook entries, read supervisor comments, view your evaluation and generate your attachment report.

Supervisors

See the students assigned to you, approve or reject each logbook entry with a comment, and submit an evaluation score.

Administrators

Assign a supervisor to each student and manage student and supervisor accounts.
